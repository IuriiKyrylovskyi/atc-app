To achieve consistent, identical coloring across images—and to make it unified for all geographical and urban features—the prompt requires precise hexadecimal (hex) color codes and specific instructions for lighting and shadows. Standard, subjective descriptions (like "warm beige" or "deep blue") will always lead to variations.

Here is a master template for a unified prompt that you can use to apply this specific color theme to _any_ location (New York City, a mountain range, a desert village, or London).

### The Unified Master Prompt:

---

> **Prompt:** An high-resolution, high-angle intricate 3D physical diorama-style map of **`[INSERT NEW LOCATION (e.g., Central Park, Manhattan, or Peruvian Andes)]`**. This is a tangible relief map, not a flat image. All colors and textures are precise and highly saturated under direct, directional light.
>
> **CRITICAL COLOR PALETTE INSTRUCTIONS (Use Exactly):**
>
> - **Primary Ground/Urban Color:** (A warm, sandy-peach/terracotta) `Hex: #EBCAB1`
> - **Primary Water Color:** (A highly saturated, radiant sapphire blue) `Hex: #0077C2`
> - **Primary Road/Street Color:** (A medium-neutral gray) `Hex: #8C8C8C`
> - **Primary Vegetation Color:** (Rich, dense forest green) `Hex: #3A6930`
> - **Landmark Accent Color:** (Deep, polished ochre/gold) `Hex: #D4AF37` (Apply to major domes, bridges, or unique peaks).
> - **Background Map Canvas Color:** (Very light, warm sand/cream) `Hex: #F2E8D5`
> - **Special Detail:** Add small, precise clusters of floral accents using pink `Hex: #FFB6C1` and purple `Hex: #9370DB` interspersed within vegetation.
>
> **Lighing & Shadow Instructions:**
>
> - **Shadow Technique:** Apply strong, crisp, dark, directional shadows (falling strictly to the bottom-right). This is essential to create the 3D depth of buildings, hills, mountains, and trees.
> - **Water Depth:** The water is deep and dark at the edges (`Hex: #004D7C`), transitioning to the radiant blue (`Hex: #0077C2`) toward the center, creating a sense of volume.
>
> **Unified Feature Key:**
>
> - _Urban Features (Cities, Streets, Squares):_ Buildings use the Ground Color (`#EBCAB1`); roads use the Road Gray (`#8C8C8C`). Squares and plazas should be clearly defined gray patches.
> - _Terrain (Hills, Mountains, fields):_ Use the Ground Color (`#EBCAB1`), but make the hills/mountains significantly physically higher and cast longer shadows. Fields should be large, flatter sections of the Ground Color.
> - _Water Features (Rivers, Seas, Oceans, Coastlines):_ All are the radiant Sapphire Blue (`#0077C2`), with deep-set, dark-edged banks. Use the Landmark Accent Color (`#D4AF37`) sparingly for major bridges.
> - _Green Spaces (Trees, Grass, Parks):_ Dense, clustered Vegetation Green (`#3A6930`) with the pink and purple floral accents.
