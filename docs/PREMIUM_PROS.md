# ATC-APP Premium — What You Get

A one-time lifetime purchase. No subscription, no recurring charges. After you
pay once, you own every feature below forever — on every device you restore to.

---

## 1. No Ads

- Interstitial ads between shifts are removed entirely.
- No banner ads, no rewarded-video prompts, no ad refresh timers.
- Free users see ads; premium users never do.

## 2. Saved Scores (Cross-Device Progress)

- Your best plane count for every level is synced to the server.
- Scores survive app restarts, device switches, and reinstalls.
- On a new device, enter your alias + password once and your whole history
  follows you. Keep them safe: they are the way to restore on every platform,
  and on Android the only reliable one.
- Free users' scores are local-only and lost if the device is reset.

## 3. All Levels Unlocked

- Free users are capped at the first 15 levels.
- Premium unlocks every level across all 20+ airports, including the endless
  shift on each airport.
- No level gating, no paywalls mid-gameplay.

## 4. New Airport Voting (Weekly, Premium-Only)

- Premium users get **one vote per week** to propose a new airport for the game.
- Votes are tallied server-side; the top proposals enter the development queue.
- Free users can view the leaderboard and proposals but cannot vote.

## 5. Propose a New Airport (Every 2 Months, Premium-Only)

- Premium users may submit **one new airport proposal every 2 months**.
- Proposals include ICAO code, region, and a short description.
- Approved proposals are built into a future release and credited to the
  proposer in the changelog.
- Free users cannot submit proposals.

## 6. Priority Feedback Channel

- Premium users' bug reports and feature requests are tagged `premium` and
  handled ahead of free-user reports in the triage queue.

## 7. No Tracking

- Premium users are never shown targeted ads (there are none anyway) and are
  never asked for tracking permission on behalf of ad networks.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account and progress in the app (Settings, Danger Zone). Your
store purchase stays with your Apple ID or Google account, so you can unlock
premium again later by restoring it with a new alias and password. On Android,
if premium does not come back, contact support and keep the Google Play order
number (it starts with GPA) from your purchase email.

See [LOGIN.md](./LOGIN.md) for the full cross-device restore flow. Use the public
